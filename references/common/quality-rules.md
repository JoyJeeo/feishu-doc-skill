# Quality rules

Use these rules when preparing or verifying user-facing content.

## Preserve meaning

- Do not invent owners, dates, metrics, decisions, or sources.
- Separate source facts, derived conclusions, and recommendations.
- Mark missing critical information as pending confirmation.
- Preserve unsupported blocks or fields instead of silently dropping them.

## Preserve scope

- Modify only the section, block, range, records, or node declared in the preview.
- Treat same-named sections as ambiguous until block identity or title path resolves them.
- Record intentional structural moves in the preview.

## Produce usable output

- Match the requested audience, document type, and language.
- Keep headings, terminology, dates, statuses, and table fields consistent.
- For large changes, summarize impact and provide representative differences without hiding the total affected scope.

## Verify results

- Compare the declared target and scope with the reread result.
- Verify required content, structure, and links.
- Check that non-target content remains unchanged where the operation promises local editing.
- Report unsupported formatting or uncertain results instead of claiming full success.
